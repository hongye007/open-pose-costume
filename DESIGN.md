# Reference studio

Audience: illustrators and character designers. Job: find a pose or outfit, inspect it, and keep a useful pair together.

Palette: studio fog #f4f6f8, paper #ffffff, graphite #232a37, pencil #737c8e, drafting blue #3d5bd9, lavender stock #eceafa. Display: Georgia with Songti SC for restrained italic editorial accents. Body: system sans with PingFang SC. Utility: monospace for stable source IDs.

Layout: a quiet 232px reference-index sidebar, broad workspace, short introductory shelf, search, category chips, then four columns of large image sheets. Mobile collapses the index above a two-column grid. A blue drafting tab and faint corner crop marks make the image sheets feel like a reference archive. The primary signature is the two-part pose/outfit study on the intro shelf; surrounding UI stays restrained.

Plan critique: avoid turning the index into a generic promotional landing page. Keep the introduction compact, let actual references occupy most of the viewport, use real category counts and source IDs, and do not decorate with invented stats. Large images and useful multi-view controls take priority over ornamental animation.
