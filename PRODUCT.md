# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Illustrators, character designers and AI creators who browse bilingual references and combine poses, outfits and optional character identities.

## Product Purpose

Bring reference discovery, favorites, image generation and comparison into one workspace. Users choose references, configure their own image service and iterate on a character image.

## Brand Personality

Clear, focused and approachable. Use concise Chinese and English labels and let reference images lead.

## Anti-references

Avoid black image backgrounds, oversized controls, overlays that obscure reference images and modal filters that disappear while browsing. Preserve the existing light visual identity and sidebar navigation.

## Design Principles

- Keep references prominent and controls compact.
- Retain search and filters while users browse long lists.
- Make selected references and service requirements clear.
- Preserve the current result while users explore another combination.
- Explain local storage and image-service requests accurately.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Retain keyboard access, visible focus, labeled controls, native dialog dismissal and responsive layouts. Support Chinese and English and respect reduced-motion preferences.
